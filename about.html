About Once Upon a Page

Welcome to Once Upon a Page.
A place for readers to discover stories worth reading and worlds worth getting lost in. Step into a cozy little corner filled with novels, timeless classics, children's stories, and light novels.
From captivating novels and timeless classics to children's books and light novels, our collection brings together stories for every kind of reader.
Whether you're searching for your next favorite book or simply looking for something new to explore, there's always a story waiting for you.

Browse different genres, discover new titles, save your favorites, and preview each book through its synopsis and first chapter before diving in.

Your next favorite story is just a page away!
